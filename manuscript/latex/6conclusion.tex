\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

In this paper, we formulated and resolved the intensely coupled joint resource allocation challenge inherent to Beyond 5G (B5G) urban vehicular edge computing networks. By reformulating the Non-deterministic Polynomial-time (NP)-hard mixed-integer non-linear programming (MINLP) optimization problem into a tractable, dual-timescale Decentralized Partially Observable Markov Decision Process (Dec-POMDP), we laid the groundwork for robust, scalable algorithmic convergence.

To overcome the dimensionality and spatial ignorance of traditional flat Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL) paradigms, we proposed a novel Hierarchical Graph Attention Multi-Agent Soft Actor-Critic (HiGAT-MASAC) architecture. Our system physically disentangled the infrastructural resource envelope distributions (governed by macro-RSU agents) from the high-speed local offloading and power control decisions (governed by micro-vehicle agents). Furthermore, the native integration of Multi-Head Graph Attention Networks (GAT) enabled the continuous Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) policies to organically encode transient Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) interference topologies into highly expressive state embeddings, entirely bypassing the fragility of manually engineered heuristic matrices.

Through an extensive suite of realistic, 3GPP-standardized Manhattan Grid mobility simulations, we unequivocally demonstrated the operational superiority of HiGAT-MASAC over contemporary state-of-the-art multi-agent baselines such as MAPPO, MADDPG, and GNN-DDQN. Consistently capturing the Pareto frontier, the proposed framework reduced mandatory execution delays by upwards of $33\%$, strictly enforcing hardware time-bounds even amidst severe network densification ($N \ge 80$). Concurrently, the topology-aware offloading ratios minimized local battery consumption by over $36\%$ while practically doubling sum-rate V2I channel throughputs compared to non-hierarchical standards.

Future research expansions will explore extending this decentralized framework to 3D mobility matrices, specifically targeting Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV)-assisted vehicular networks and low-Earth orbit (LEO) satellite non-terrestrial integration, to guarantee ubiquitous B5G connectivity across severely disconnected rural terrains.
